SQLens
Watch your data transform.

What this version assumes

  • Tables update as you type. The result and the steps appear when you press Run.
  • Run changes the data for real, like any SQL client. On the sample database, Reset puts it back.
  • Tables show the first 100 rows. Show, above the tables, raises it to 500 or 1000.
  • To draw the steps, the app makes short-lived temporary tables on your connection. They are removed when it finishes.
  • A window function in the same query as GROUP BY still runs and shows a result, but its steps are not drawn yet.
  • History, settings and your draft query are kept on this computer only.
